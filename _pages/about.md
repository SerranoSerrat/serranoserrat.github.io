I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute for Public Goods and Policies (IPP) of the Spanish National Research Council (CSIC).

My research interests are in the field of political economy. In this area, I am mainly concerned with the determinants of political preferences. Currently, I work on the ERC-funded research project CONSENT, where we analyze the motivations of losers to consent to being ruled by the winners.

My work has been accepted at the Journal of Politics, World Politics, and the European Journal of Political Research, among others.

On this website, you can find my [CV](/files/cv.pdf), [Research](/research/), and [Software](/software/).

If you would like to get in touch and talk about projects or common interests, please feel free to contact me at j.serrano.serrat@csic.es

Thank you for visiting!
